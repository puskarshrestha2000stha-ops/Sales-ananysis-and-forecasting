Product Sales Data
Overview

This dataset contains product sales information used to analyze sales performance, revenue, product demand, and customer purchasing trends.

Dataset Contents

The data may include the following fields:

Product ID — Unique identifier for each product.

Product Name — Name of the product.

Category — Product category or department.

Sales Date — Date when the sale occurred.

Quantity Sold — Number of units sold.

Unit Price — Selling price per unit.

Total Sales — Total revenue generated from the transaction.

Customer ID — Identifier for the customer, where available.

Region — Geographic region associated with the sale.

Purpose

The dataset can be used to:

Monitor product and category sales performance.

Identify high- and low-performing products.

Analyze revenue trends over time.

Understand purchasing patterns.

Compare sales across regions or categories.

Support business reporting and decision-making.

Data Preparation

Before analysis, the data should be checked for:

Missing or duplicate records.

Invalid dates or product identifiers.

Incorrect quantities or prices.

Inconsistent category or region names.

Potential outliers in sales values.

Key Metrics

Common metrics that can be calculated include:

Total revenue

Total units sold

Average order value

Average selling price

Revenue by product

Revenue by category

Monthly or yearly sales

Product sales growth
